Unit 8: World Heritage

The Shat Gambuj Mosque (Bagerhat)

  • Founded by Khan Jahan Ali
  • 60 domes (77 total domes, 60 pillars)
  • Tughlaq architecture
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site

The Statue of Liberty

  • Gift from France to USA
  • Designed by Frédéric-Auguste Bartholdi
  • Symbol of freedom and democracy
  • Located in New York
