৩. Right Forms of Verbs

Master Rules

Rule 1: Habitual fact / Universal truth

সবসময় Present Simple Tense হয়।

Example: The sun rises in the east.

Rule 2: Adverbs of Time (Tense Markers)

  • Present Continuous: now, at present, at this moment
  • Present Perfect: just, just now, already, recently, lately
  • Past Simple: yesterday, ago, long since, last night, in 1971
  • Present Perfect Continuous: for, since (কিছু সময় ধরে)

Rule 3: Preposition + Verb+ing

Preposition (for, of, in, with, without, by) + (Verb + ing)

Rule 4: Modal + Base Form

can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must, had better + V₁

Rule 5: To-infinitive

to + V₁ (base form)
