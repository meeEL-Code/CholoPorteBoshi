৬. Tag Questions

মূল নিয়ম

  • বাক্য Affirmative হলে Tag Negative
  • বাক্য Negative হলে Tag Affirmative

Imperative Sentence

  • Let's / Let us: Tag → shall we?
  • Let him / Let them / Order / Request: Tag → will you?

Subject Replacement Rule

  • Everybody, Everyone, Somebody, Nobody, No one, Neither: Tag Subject → they
  • Everything, Something, Nothing, Anything: Tag Subject → it

Negative অর্থ প্রকাশকারী শব্দ (Tag Positive হবে)

Seldom, Hardly, Scarcely, Few, Little, Barely, Rarely
