২. Prepositions

Common Prepositions

in, on, at, by, with, from, to, of, for, about, into, over, under

Time Rules

  • At: নির্দিষ্ট সময় (at 5 pm, at night)
  • On: দিন/তারিখ (on Monday, on 26 March)
  • In: মাস/বছর/সময়কাল (in May, in 1971, in the morning)

Preposition + Verb+ing

  • Look forward to + V-ing
  • With a view to + V-ing
  • Mind + V-ing
  • Worth + V-ing
  • Get used to + V-ing
