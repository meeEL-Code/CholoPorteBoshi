৫. Narration

Direct → Indirect

  • Comma + inverted comma → that / if / whether
  • Say → say; Say to → tell
  • Tense back-shift (এক ধাপ পিছিয়ে)
  • Pronoun change
  • Time/place word change: now → then, today → that day, here → there
