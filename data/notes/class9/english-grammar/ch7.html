Graph & Chart Writing Structure

  • Para 1 (Intro): The graph/chart illustrates/shows the percentage/number of... from [year] to [year].
  • Para 2 (Main Trends): Highlighting highest point, lowest point, comparisons
  • Para 3 (Conclusion): In conclusion / To sum up, it is evident that...

Useful Vocabulary

Increased rapidly, plummeted/decreased sharply, remained constant, fluctuated, reached the peak of
