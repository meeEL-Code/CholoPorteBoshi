৮. Punctuation & Capitalization

Marks

  • Full stop (.)
  • Comma (,)
  • Question mark (?)
  • Exclamation mark (!)
  • Quotation marks (" ")
  • Colon (:)
  • Semicolon (;)
  • Apostrophe (')

Capitalization Rules

  • Sentence শুরুতে Capital letter
  • Proper noun → Capital (Dhaka, Bangladesh, Rahim)
  • "I" সর্বদা capital
  • Days, months → Capital (Monday, January)
  • Nationalities, languages → Capital (Bangladeshi, Bangla)
